## Relevant evidence and exclusions

- The selected local-Jackal scene composes one static triangle terrain collider. `Cube_003` remains inactive; older scenes that activate it were moved to the separately preserved candidate archive.
- `ros2 param dump` on the live legacy nodes observed Joy driver `device_id=0`, deadzone 0.05, autorepeat 20 Hz, coalesce 1 ms, sticky buttons false; teleop observed 20 Hz, 0.1 deadzone and cubic scaling. The old mapping reads Joy axes 1 and 3.
- The canonical scene points to the local VLP-16 USD and maps the four graph tokens to `front_left_wheel`, `front_right_wheel`, `rear_left_wheel`, and `rear_right_wheel`.
- Generic terrain generators, lunar-yard worlds, high-resolution DEM acquisition tools, A* planners, CAN/micro-ROS firmware, MCP/LLM integrations, and other robot assets remain in the adjacent source archive. None are active dependencies of this simulation.
